New here? How to read this page

Every AI promise here is checked against evidence and turned into a signed receipt, like the inspection sticker in an elevator. Green means the promise held. Amber means a named human must look. Red means stop.

PASS promise kept, evidence accepted   REVIEW paused for a human decision   FAIL promise missed   BLOCK hard stop, do not proceed

Where a number comes from matters: measured here, published by a named source, quoted by a vendor, or assumed. A vendor quote can pause a check even when the number looks good, because a quote is not a measurement.

Buttons: Verify chain re-checks every record. Preview tamper shows what one edited digit does. Nothing leaves your machine.
