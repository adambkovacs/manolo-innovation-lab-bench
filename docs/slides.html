Approach

Every verdict becomes a signed, replayable receipt

bench measures→ evidence + declared origin→ gate→ verdict→ Ed25519 receipt, chained→ ledger
  • Origins before arithmetic: measured, published, upstream, assumed. A threshold match from an unaccepted origin is REVIEW, never PASS.
  • Dispositions: PASS→CONTINUE, REVIEW and FAIL→PAUSE, BLOCK→STOP.
  • Oversight: only REVIEW is overridable, by a named actor with reason and scope, and the override is itself a signed receipt. Integrity failures are never overridable.

Limitations, read plainly

We score ourselves REVIEW where we are REVIEW

  • Claims here are self-declared, about our own system. The value for MANOLO is the contract applied to their table, with use-case owners declaring thresholds.
  • Single local keypair, no PKI. Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof: the mitigation is the published chain-head hash.
  • Energy is not metered in joules. Our own energy claim pauses itself instead of pretending.
  • Synthetic seeded corpus; recall on real embedding distributions will differ. Latency crosses runtimes, as stated.

The rest of these slides is only as credible as these self-scores.
